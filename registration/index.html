Registration

Registration for the 5th NLP Symposium is free. Please fill out the form below to reserve your seat; space may be limited, so early registration is encouraged. You will receive a confirmation email once your registration is processed.

In the meantime, you can register by emailing your name, affiliation, and area of interest to caifs@isra.edu.pk with the subject line “5th NLP Symposium Registration”.

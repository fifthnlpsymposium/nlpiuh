Welcome to the 5th NLP Symposium

The 5th NLP Symposium brings together researchers, students, and practitioners working on Natural Language Processing, with a special focus on low-resource South Asian languages such as Sindhi and Urdu. The symposium is organized by the Department of Computer Science, Isra University Hyderabad, and will be held on Thursday, September 17, 2026.

The day will feature keynote talks, research presentations, hands-on workshops, and a poster/demo session covering topics such as language modeling for low-resource languages, corpus and treebank construction, machine translation, speech processing, and applications of large language models to regional languages. See the Program page for the full schedule, and the Registration page to reserve your seat.
